Sync for Payroll overview

Manage payroll reconciliation with your customers' accounting software

Use Codat to integrate your customers' payroll data from their HR and payroll platforms into their accounting software and to support its reconciliation.

We can support HR and payroll software software companies and vertical SaaS companies in their activity by leveraging our Sync for Payroll API solution in response to customers' requests for accounting integrations.
