Sync for Payroll

Embedded accounting automation that supplies everything payroll providers need to deliver high-quality accounting integrations for customers, fast.

Use Codat to integrate your customers' payroll data from their HR and payroll platforms into their accounting software and to support its reconciliation.

How Codat helps​

Sync for Payroll supports HR, payroll, and vertical SaaS companies integrate their product with their customers' accounting software.

Integrating with Codat helps your SMBSMB The primary customer segment that Codat helps businesses serve, typically companies with annual revenues under $500 million. customers remove complexity and repetition from payroll processing, eliminate manual entry and mapping of payroll data, and save time on tasks that usually need to be completed multiple times a month.

How it works​

Connect to your SMB's accounting software​

Codat handles authorization and linking to your SMBSMB The primary customer segment that Codat helps businesses serve, typically companies with annual revenues under $500 million.'s accounting software and takes into account intricate details such as rate limits.

Categorize and map payroll components​

Each business may need to reconcile payroll costs, pensions, and taxes to different nominal accounts, based on their business type and the accounting software they use. Codat provides an endpoint to read chart of accounts and tracking categories data in order to avoid manual categorization efforts and give flexibility to SMBsSMB The primary customer segment that Codat helps businesses serve, typically companies with annual revenues under $500 million. for reconciling data. As a payroll platform, you will need to build a flexible interface for your customer to perform the mapping.

Sync payruns with your customers accounting software​

Use Codat's create and update functionality to post a journal showing the correct amounts against each account. These transactions can easily be reconciled against the business's bank feed.
