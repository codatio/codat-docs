Writing historic transactions​

You can write historic transactions up to seven days old based on the feed start date chosen by the SMBSMB The primary customer segment that Codat helps businesses serve, typically companies with annual revenues under $500 million. user in the QBO UI. All bank transactions must be cleared and have a clearedOnDate set to the current or the prior day.

Writing future (future-dated) bank transactions to QBO is not supported.
